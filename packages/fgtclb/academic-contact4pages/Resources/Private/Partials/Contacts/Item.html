One contact of the page, as "Contacts/List" renders every one of them: in a role group, below the groups for a contact without a role, and in the list that is not grouped. A project that wants its own card overrides this partial rather than the list template. The grid column around it belongs to the list template. Arguments: - contact: the contact - role: the role of the contact, or null when it has none - profile: the profile behind the contract of the contact - contract: the contract the contact names - settings: the plugin settings - data: the content element record, as an array - grouped: set when the contact renders below the heading of its role

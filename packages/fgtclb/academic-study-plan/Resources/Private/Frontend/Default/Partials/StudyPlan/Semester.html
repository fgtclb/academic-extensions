One semester column of the study plan. Arguments: - semester: the semester record, with its `modules`. - idPrefix: what the dialog ids of the plan start with, passed on to the modules. Empty for a plan placed on the page. The header is the accordion control on a narrow viewport and inert on a wide one, which is why it is rendered `aria-hidden` and `inert`: the script removes both as soon as it turns the column into an accordion.

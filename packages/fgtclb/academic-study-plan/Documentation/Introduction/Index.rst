..  _introduction:

What does it do?
================

The extension adds one content element, the study plan. It shows the course of
a study programme: the semesters side by side, and below each semester the
modules taught in it, with their credit points and notes. Visitors highlight
the modules of a category with a filter above the plan, open the description
of a module in a dialog, and read every semester as an accordion on a phone.

Editors enter the semesters and their modules as inline records of the content
element, so the plan lives where it is shown, usually on the page of the study
programme. See :ref:`integrator-study-plan` for the fields and
:ref:`styling` for the classes a site package styles the plan through.

`table-list`, drawn in a fixed grey instead of the colour of the surrounding
text, so that it reads on a light and a dark page alike.

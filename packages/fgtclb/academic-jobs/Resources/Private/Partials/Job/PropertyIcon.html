The glyph in front of a job property, by the name of the Extbase property. One map for the list, the detail view and the contact block. A property that is not in it renders no icon, so a property added to an overridden list does not show the "icon not found" placeholder. Override this partial to change the map; replace a glyph everywhere by registering its identifier again in the Configuration/Icons.php of a site package.

One timeline entry. `date`, `dateStart` and `dateEnd` are calendar dates (or null) and are rendered as a single date, a range "2018 – 2024", or an open range prefixed with the "since" / "till" label. How much of each date a visitor sees - the year alone, the year and the month, or all of it - is the configuration of the entry's own section, and the parts that are shown are formatted for the locale of the matched site language. The ViewHelper resolves both from the record type, so this template names no format.

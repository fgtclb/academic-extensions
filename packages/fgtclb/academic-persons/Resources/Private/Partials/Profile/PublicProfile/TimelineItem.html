One timeline entry. `date`, `dateStart` and `dateEnd` are DateTime objects (or null) and are formatted for the locale of the requested site language: an entry flagged `yearOnly` shows the four digit year, every other one the medium date of that locale - "Jan 5, 2024" on an English site, "05.01.2024" on a German one. `pattern` goes through the ICU formatter, which is what makes the site locale decide; `format` would be a fixed PHP date format.
